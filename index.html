About

My name is Haneol Lee — Roy in English. I work across ECG, speech, and video — but the common thread is always the same: the kind of noisy, real-world signal that clinicians, product teams, and courtrooms can actually act on.

Most of my career has been split between national R&D projects and production teams, which means I'm equally comfortable writing a research proposal and shipping the API endpoint that follows it.

I lean generalist by design. Before AI was in my job title, I taught myself immunology and drug discovery from textbooks and primary literature, alone, with no LLM to lean on. That project turned into a PhD offer from a structural biology professor in Boston and a win at a national AI drug discovery competition. The habit stuck: I still learn a new domain top-down, starting from the target task and working backward into whatever I need to know to hit it.
