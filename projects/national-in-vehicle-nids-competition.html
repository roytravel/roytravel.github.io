Security · Korea University · with IITP

National In-Vehicle N-IDS Competition

Korea University · Apr 2020 – Dec 2020

Overview

With autonomous vehicles came a new attack surface: the CAN bus. I organized a nationwide competition to spur research on in-vehicle network intrusion detection, funded by Korea's IITP.

I generated the attack/defense datasets by analyzing CAN protocol structure and injecting attack packets in Python, evaluated the anomaly-detection submissions from qualifying and final-round teams, then co-authored the resulting paper and published the dataset publicly.
