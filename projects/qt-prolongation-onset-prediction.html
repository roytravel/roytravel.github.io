QT Prolongation Onset Prediction

Synergy AI · Jun – Jul 2026

Overview

Long QT Syndrome is dangerous precisely because it's silent until it isn't — a prolonged QT interval can trigger a fatal arrhythmia with little warning. This project built a short-term risk model that flags onset 1, 2, or 3 days ahead directly from ECG signals.

Raw signals were filtered using QTc and heart-rate thresholds, automated-reading-based diagnosis confirmation, and confounder removal, before training. I benchmarked a standard ResNet against a Quantum CNN architecture on the same task, and tuned the final model deliberately as a high-sensitivity rule-out tool — its job is to make sure nothing dangerous slips through, not to make a final diagnosis on its own.

Results

AUROC 0.85NPV 0.991–3 day horizon
