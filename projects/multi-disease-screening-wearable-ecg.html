Healthcare AI · Synergy AI

Multi-Disease Screening from Wearable ECG

Synergy AI · Sep 2025 – Present

Overview

A smartwatch ECG captures far more than heart rhythm — with the right model, it can hint at conditions that normally require a blood draw. This project built the pipeline to screen for 10 major clinical conditions (diabetes, chronic kidney disease, sepsis, and more) directly from wearable ECG signals.

The pipeline covers the full lifecycle: signal pretraining, fine-tuning, and internal/external cohort validation. Most conditions reached AUROC, Sensitivity, and Specificity above 0.80 — strong enough that IRB documentation was drafted and submitted, and a clinical paper is in progress with internal-medicine physicians.

One signal, 30 biomarkers

Rather than predicting a single disease label, the model extracts 30 continuous clinical values, from HbA1c and eGFR to K+ and NT-proBNP, simultaneously from one 30-second wearable ECG.

A single non-invasive ECG stream standing in for a 30-value blood panel

Clinical scope

Those 30 biomarkers roll up into 9 clinical domains, each flagged from its own biomarker cluster.

Glycemic, liver, metabolic, kidney, oxygen delivery, electrolyte, cardiac, coagulation, and immune & inflammation, all from one neural network pass

Lead-agnostic by design

Hospital ECGs use a standard 12-lead setup. A smartwatch or other wearable records only a single lead. A model trained on 12-lead data alone would likely break the moment it saw a 1-lead signal in the real world.

Random lead selection during training forces the model to learn signal features that hold up regardless of how many leads it's given

1-lead to 12-lead reconstruction

For cases where only a single wearable lead is available, a second model generates the missing 11 leads before handing the reconstructed 12-lead tensor to the biomarker prediction pipeline. Training and inference on the reconstructed signal outperformed running the raw 1-lead signal alone.

A generative model synthesizes the missing 11 leads from a single wearable channel before biomarker prediction runs
