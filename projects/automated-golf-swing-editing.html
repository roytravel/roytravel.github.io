Automated Golf Swing Editing

Ellexi · May 2023 – Jul 2023 · with Sure Mobility

Overview

Editing golf highlight reels by hand — scrubbing through footage to find the Setup-to-Impact window — doesn't scale. I built a YOWO-based action-localization model to detect that phase of a golf swing automatically for client Sure Mobility.

Vision alone missed some edge cases, so I fused audio-peak detection (the distinct sound of club-to-ball contact) with the visual model, which measurably improved timing accuracy over either signal alone.
