Speech AI · Synergy AI

Low-Latency Clinical Speech Recognition

Synergy AI · Jan – Jun 2025

Overview

HyperMedicus, Synergy AI's clinical STT product, needed to transcribe urology doctor–patient dialogue accurately and fast enough for real-time use. I fine-tuned Whisper-large-v3 with LoRA on that specific dialogue domain, where standard STT models tend to stumble on clinical terminology.

For deployment, the model was quantized to FP16 via CTranslate2 and served with Faster-Whisper, bringing inference latency under 2 seconds. The model passed official Korea Telecommunications Technology Association (TTA) certification with a CER of 0.09 and WER of 0.12.
