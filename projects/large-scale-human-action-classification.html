Large-Scale Human Action Classification

Ellexi · Jan 2023 – May 2023

Overview

Before a public dataset goes out on AI-HUB, someone has to verify it's actually learnable. This project validated a 2-/3-person action classification dataset by training models on it and confirming the labels held up.

I trained on a 1.2TB video dataset across 8 GPUs (4× A100, 4× V100), reaching mAP of 80.64% for 2-person actions and 84.69% for 3-person actions, then Dockerized the inference pipeline for official TTA validation.
