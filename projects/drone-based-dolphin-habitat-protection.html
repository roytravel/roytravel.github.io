Drone-Based Dolphin Habitat Protection

MODULABS · Aug 2023 – Jan 2024

Overview

Off the coast of Jeju, tourism vessels have been encroaching on the habitat of the endangered Indo-Pacific bottlenose dolphin — activity that Korean maritime ecosystem law prohibits, but which is hard to prove without evidence. I trained ByteTrack object-tracking models on drone footage to monitor vessel activity around the habitat.

The tracking output became the evidentiary basis referenced in maritime law enforcement discussions. The work was published at IEEE CAI 2024, presented at the Kakao Impact Conference and MODUCON 2023, and covered by Korean tech and general media.
