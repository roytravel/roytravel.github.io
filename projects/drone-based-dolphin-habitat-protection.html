Drone-Based Dolphin Habitat Protection

MODULABS · Aug 2023 – Jan 2024 · 6 months

Overview

The Indo-Pacific bottlenose dolphin (Tursiops aduncus) is a protected species endemic to Jeju Island's waters. Korea's Marine Ecosystem Act prohibits tourism vessels from approaching within 50m of dolphin pods — but even when violations were clearly visible in drone footage, there was no quantitative method to prove the actual distance. Eyeballing aerial video is legally insufficient; without a measurable number, enforcement has no ground to stand on.

Working with Kakao Impact, the Jeju Provincial Government, and MARC across 20 weeks, I built a drone-based AI monitoring system using ByteTrack for multi-object tracking. The system detects vessels and dolphins from live drone footage and calculates real-time physical distances in meters — converting visual evidence into precise, legally defensible measurements. The work was published at IEEE CAI 2024 and presented at the Kakao Impact Conference and MODUCON 2023.

Takeaways

Ask for help early when you're stuck
Time lost struggling alone is time the team doesn't get back — reaching out to a teammate quickly is always the right call.
Keeping a team together requires genuine commitment from the leader and visible social impact
People stay when they see the leader caring deeply and when the project demonstrably creates value for society — not just for the résumé.
Diverse backgrounds mean unequal domain understanding — communication takes extra care
When engineers, researchers, and domain experts work together, the baseline for what's "obvious" shifts per person. Assuming shared context is the fastest way to misalign.
Good work leads to referrals and job offers through colleagues
With the right skills and reputation, introductions and opportunities come naturally through the people you've worked alongside.
For complex implementations, reach for open source first
ByteTrack, SAHI, and other existing tools covered the hardest parts — building from scratch would have consumed the entire timeline.
Group labeling requires a labeling policy
When many people label data simultaneously without a shared standard, inconsistencies compound fast — defining the policy before starting saves far more time than fixing it after.
Log hyperparameter experiments with W&B
Weights & Biases makes experiment tracking effortless — without it, reproducing or comparing runs becomes a manual headache.
