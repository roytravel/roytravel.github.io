MHC–Peptide Binding Affinity Prediction

Personal Project · Apr – May 2022

Overview

A month-long, self-directed detour outside my formal domain: could I learn enough computational immunology from scratch to contribute something real to early-stage drug discovery? I modeled peptide-protein binding affinity to help shrink the drug candidate search space.

Using BLOSUM62 matrix encoding for peptide/protein sequence features and DeepAttentionPan for binding prediction, the project won the Grand Prize at the 2024 AI Drug Discovery Idea Competition hosted by K-Medihub, and led to a Ph.D. admissions offer from a structural biology professor in Boston.
