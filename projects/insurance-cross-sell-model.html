Revenue-Driving Insurance Cross-Sell Model

Ellexi · May – Oct 2024

Overview

Kyobo Life Insurance wanted to know which existing customers were likely to buy additional coverage. Getting there started with the unglamorous part: writing over 1,000 lines of Oracle SQL to build the feature tables from raw customer data.

From those features, I trained and compared XGBoost, LightGBM, and CatBoost models to predict cross-sell propensity. Forward testing — running the model against real, subsequent customer behavior — confirmed it drove an actual revenue increase, not just a good offline metric.
