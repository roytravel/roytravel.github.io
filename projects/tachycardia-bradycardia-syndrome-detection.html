Tachycardia-Bradycardia Syndrome (TBS) Detection

Synergy AI · Feb 2026

Overview

Most Holter monitor reports exist only as scanned PDFs — unusable for model training as-is. The first step here was making that data usable: digitizing scanned Holter analysis reports with Tesseract OCR and extracting key-value pairs into structured JSON.

From there, I built a clinical labeling pipeline that flags tachycardia episodes from ECG signals using a max RR-interval threshold, while explicitly excluding confounding diagnoses like Sick Sinus Syndrome and Complete AV Block through ICD-code-based filtering — the kind of clinical nuance that keeps a dataset trustworthy.
