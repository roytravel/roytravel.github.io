3D Facial Mesh Generation (3DFaceCAM)

Ellexi · Jul 2023 – Feb 2024 · with NIA

Overview

This national R&D project, in partnership with Korea's National Information Society Agency (NIA), trained a 3D facial mesh generation model on Korean faces for public release on AI-HUB — filling a gap in Korean-specific 3D face datasets available for research.

I wrote the technical R&D proposal that won the project, trained 3DFaceCAM on 4× A100 80GB GPUs, and built the Docker-based evaluation pipeline used for official TTA certification, reaching a Diversity score of 1.33 and Specificity of 0.90.
